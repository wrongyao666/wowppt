WowPPT Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

1. Overview

WowPPT is a personal publishing tool operated by an individual (the account owner). It publishes the owner's own self-created business presentation template showcase videos to the owner's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

2. Information We Access

With the owner's authorization via TikTok Login Kit, WowPPT accesses basic TikTok account information (scope: user.info.basic) and publishes videos to the owner's account (scope: video.publish). WowPPT does not collect information about any other TikTok users.

3. How Information Is Used

Account information and access tokens are used solely to authenticate and to publish the owner's videos to the owner's own account. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. No advertising, tracking, or analytics are performed.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in secure credential storage and are used only for the purposes above. Video files published through WowPPT are created by the owner.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The owner may revoke WowPPT's access at any time from their TikTok account settings, which invalidates the tokens. To request deletion of any stored data, contact us by email.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
